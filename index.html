For You

How I Help You Stand Out

Recruiting rewards the people who show up prepared, visible, and impossible to forget. These are the systems that get you there — built for you, not handed to you as homework.

Stand Out in Recruiting

Recruiters only spend a few seconds looking at your resume. I help you build a strong and consistent presence across your resume, LinkedIn, and everything else recruiters might find so you actually stand out.

A Personal Site That Sells You

A personal website built around your experience, projects, and what you actually bring to the table. One link that gives recruiters a better idea of who you are before you even get on the call.

Outreach on Autopilot

Find the right people and companies to reach out to, then make the process easier with personalized outreach. Research who you’re contacting, what they do, and why you’re reaching out so every message actually feels personal.

Job Search, Made Simple

Finding the right roles, getting your applications out, and keeping track of everything without making the process a full-time job. I help you stay organized so you can focus on the opportunities that actually make sense for you.

A Network That Never Forgets

Keep track of the people you meet, what you talked about, and the things that matter to them. Instead of forgetting about a conversation six months later, you’ll know exactly what to follow up on and how to keep the relationship going.

Interview Prep That Lands

Practice the questions you’re actually going to get and get honest feedback on your answers. Walk into interviews knowing your stories, your numbers, and how you want to talk about your experience.

How We Work

Our Process

A methodical, low-risk approach that starts with understanding where you actually are — not jumping straight into tools.

01

Discover & Diagnose

  • We learn how your work actually gets done today
  • Understand goals, constraints, and what success looks like
  • Find where the manual work is hiding
02

  • Design around the highest-impact opportunities first
  • Prototype 2–3 approaches instead of betting on one
  • Test safely in our environment before it touches yours
03

Launch, Monitor & Optimize

  • Go live with clear success metrics
  • Continuous monitoring and support
  • Iterate based on real feedback and outcomes
